Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze is a Great Enemy Boss in Elden Ring. Onze is a demi-human that, despite his appearance, skillfully wields a magic katana and is found at the bottom of the Belurat Gaol. This is an optional boss as players don't need to defeat it to advance in Shadow of the Erdtree.

Elden Ring Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze Boss

  • This is an optional boss
  • Closest Site of Grace: Belurat Gaol
  • Multiplayer is allowed for this boss
  • You can summon Spirit Ashes for this boss

Elden Ring Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze Location

Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze can be found at the bottom of the Belurat Gaol. Players will find the entrance to this area by going to the lake east of the Belurat Tower Settlement. Players will see cages and traps along the way that will lead to a small entrance to the location. To reach the boss location, look for the section where you see crock pots suspended in the air with chains. Jump down these platforms and navigate the area until you see the yellow mist door on the south side. See the Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze on the Elden Ring Shadow of the Erdtree Map [Video Link]

Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze Combat information

  • Health: 14,883 HP
  • Defense: 121
  • Stance: 65
  • Parryable: Yes
  • Is vulnerable to a critical hit after being stance broken or parried
  • Damage: Slash, Pierce, Magic
  • Inflicts: Frostbite
  • Drops 80,000, Demi-Human Swordsman Yosh

Negations (or Absorptions)

  • Standard: 0
  • Slash: -20
  • Strike: 0
  • Pierce: 0
  • Magic: 0
  • Fire: -20
  • Lightning: 0
  • Holy: 0
The negation numbers are the % of your damage that gets blocked. For example, if a negation is 60, 40% of that damage by that type will go through and 60% will be negated. Bigger number = less damage. A negation of 100 means no damage goes through, and a negation of -100 mean the enemy takes 2x damage from that source. 0 means damage goes pretty much as is.

Resistances

  • Poison: 210 / 238 / 280 / 378 / 668 / 1125
  • Scarlet Rot: 210 / 238 / 280 / 378 / 668 / 1125
  • Hemorrhage: 280 / 322 / 420 / 710 / 1167
  • Frostbite: 280 / 322 / 420 / 710 / 1167
  • Sleep: Immune
  • Madness: Immune
The resistance numbers are the buildup amount to trigger it. For example, if a resistance is 100 you must deal 100 points of the given buildup to trigger it. Note that these go down over time, and increase each time the effect procs. The values after the "/"s indicate the increased resistances after each successive proc.

Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze Fight Strategy

The Best Tips for Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze:
  • Summons: Black Knife Tiche, Mimic Tear, Greatshield Soldier Ashes
  • Spells: Glintstone Pebble
  • Bring any Greatshield
Melee Users
The boss is a small creature that can move nimbly. They can outmaneuver you in combat and sneak up behind you with their fast movement. This boss, however, can be staggered and their poise can be broken by repeatedly hitting them. You can also summon Spirit Ashes like Black Knife Tiche. Their attacks will apply stagger and their quick movement will allow them to keep up with the boss fight. Alternatively, you can also summon Greatshield Soldier Ashes to force the boss into a corner, or you can opt for a Greatshield to absorb attacks from this boss.

While fighting this boss, make sure to keep your target locked on them so they don't sneak up on you. This also lets you be on guard for any of their attacks. Despite their quick movement, their slashes are comparatively slow because of the wind-up. He holds up his weapon for a few frames before launching the attack and lets you predict their next move. You can alternate going offense and defense by holding up your shield when he's attacking, and then attacking him when he pulls back. This should give you a few frames to attack him while he's not jumping around. This boss can easily be staggered with light and heavy attacks.

The boss will begin the fight with a move called Magic Slash. He will teleport midair in front of the player and will slash downward to bring forth magical damage in a small area. You can dodge this attack by rolling backward, or to your right. We recommend the latter to close the gap and land a free heavy attack. There's a short pause after the move that lets you hit him freely and you can cut off the rest of his attack pattern.

Another move that is worth mentioning is the Orb Cuts. He usually does this when you attempt an attack while he's guarding, and you will see him raise his sword above his head and stabs forward to create an orb around him. This generates an energy that cuts anything within the area. Similar to the Waterfowl Dance but, the boss will stay only in one place. This means that you can dodge this attack by rolling backward.

He has about 3 Magic attacks aside from his Standard Sword attacks. The magic ones are deadlier than his attacks, make sure to keep an eye on his sword if it's glowing blue, or white. When you get the chance, attempt to strike him from behind repeatedly to break his poise. With the help of any spirit summons, this fight shouldn't take long.

Magic and Ranged Users
Use spirit summons like Tiche who can keep up with Onze’s speed as he will dart around the space for the duration of the battle. While Tiche is engaged, use quick spells like Glintstone pebble. If Onze is focused on you, be patient and dodge until your spirit summons can take aggro again. He will raise his blade or jump into the air to indicate an attack is coming which makes dodging a simple task.

Onze is most vulnerable from behind and will take more damage from the back. With a spirit summons, this battle should be fairly simple.

Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze Attacks & Counters

Attack Description Counter
Parry Holds his sword in a defensive position. If an attack is blocked, he nimbly dashes to the target's sides, slashing twice.
He is invulnerable during his dash. 		Don't attack too much, or else he will use this move and get a near-guaranteed hit. He will also counter ranged attacks like spells or arrows/bolts.
Onze's Line of Stars Imbues his sword with magic, extending its reach before performing two quick slashes. He follows-up by extending its reach further, slashing twice with slight delay. Afterwards, he ends by leaping upward and slamming his sword down. Roll backward once. This lets you create distance and prevents you from taking the full brunt of the attack.
Descending Slash Onze vanishes into dust and reappears above his target, imbuing his sword with magic and slashing downwards.

He may also perform a simpler variant, jumping into the air and slashing downwards. 		As you as he disappears, watch above your head and dodge.
Sword Poke Sidesteps around target, thrusting once, vanishing, and slashing twice. He may use a follow-up. Dodge both the poke and the follow-ups.
Mist Cutter Onze's sword glows white before swinging it once, generating several slashes that surround him. This builds up Frostbite rapidly. Don't get too greedy, as he gains a decent amount of hyperarmor while charging. This attack is easily avoided by moving backwards.
Heavy Slashes Holds his weapon directly above his head before performing two quick slashes, he may combo with any other moves alongside this. Dodge or block and prepare for any follow-ups.
Chasing Slices Holds his sword to his side before attacking up to four times, moving forward as he does. The last slash is delayed. Dodge or roll the opposite that he's swinging the weapon.
Disengage Slashes once before jumping backwards. The attack comes out rather slow, so dodge it and then heal.
Swift Slices Slashes once, sidesteps around his target, then slashes upwards while backflipping away. Depending on which side he is coming from, you can roll to the opposite side to avoid getting damaged.
Grab Onze's left hand glows blue before he reaches forward with it. If it lands, he pushes his target back and traps them in a magical bubble, before reappearing in front of them and telepathically tossing their body aside, letting them fall to the ground. Dodge to the sides. He often uses this as part of his combos, so be aware.

Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze Notes & Other Trivia

  • Slash and Fire negation are both -20, so either lands 20% harder than normal — a fire-affinity slashing weapon is the ideal answer.
  • Every other damage type sits at 0 negation, so nothing is actively resisted.
  • Stance is only 65, the lowest of the DLC gaol bosses, and it is parryable on top of that.
  • 14,883 HP behind 121 defense makes it a longer fight than the stance number suggests.
  • It waits at the bottom of Belurat Gaol, entered from the lake east of the Belurat Tower Settlement past the cages and traps.

Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze Lore

Lore Items Related to Demi-Human Swordmaster Onze

Demi-Human Swordsman Yosh

Ashen remains in which spirits yet dwell. 'Use to summon the spirit of Yosh, the demi-human swordsman.

Onze, a master swordsman who devoted himself to the Star-Lined Sword, realized that only ruin awaited at the end of the procession of stars, and imprisoned himself in order to forestall it.

However, Yosh, an apprentice who absorbed his technique, stubbornly refused to heed his master's words, and spent his entire life at his side, in defiance of his self-imposed seclusion.

Star-Lined Sword

Sword encrusted with a line of stars fashioned from small pieces of crude glintstone. Weapon of the demi-human swordsmen.

When bestowed with this weapon by their queen, the swordsmen swear to find the truth that lies at the end of the procession of stars.

Demi-Human Queen's Staff

Glintstone staff styled as a scepter. A gift once given to the demi-humans to foster peace, it can be wielded even by those of low intelligence.

Sneered at by fools in the academy.

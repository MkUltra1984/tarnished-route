Lamenter's Gaol is a Location in Elden Ring, added with the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. The Lamenter's Gaol is found in southwest part of Charo's Hidden Grave region. You can reach this place by heading south down the cliffside near Ensis Castle and following the river down, or by going through Dragon's Pit and heading southwest.

Lamenter's Gaol Map

  • You can reach this area after defeating the Ancient Dragon-Man in Dragon's Pit. If you haven't defeated the boss yet, please see Dragon's Pit

Starting from the Dragon's Pit Terminus Site of Grace See it in the Elden Ring Map, go towards east and past the Jagged Peak Drake. You can avoid this fight by sprinting past it while riding Torrent. Then, turn to the west and go towards the corpse of an ancient dragon. You will arrive at the Grand Altar of Dragon Communion.

Jump towards the west behind the corpse and turn southwest. Follow the ramp going up, then drop down to the field of red flowers. Head southwest and follow the path to reach Charo's Hidden Grave. Continue heading northwest and go towards the waterfall in the distance. Go to the left side of the waterfall and climb up the ramp. Stick to the edge on the west and use the Site of Grace in Charo's Hidden Grave just before the arch.

Go through the arch and continue northwest. You will arrive at another body of water surrounded by gravestones and Gravebirds. Continue riding northwest past the Spirit Eel creatures. Once you reach the area with metal cages, stick to the mountain wall on your right and you will eventually find the cave entrance to Lamenter's Gaol. [See Shadow of the Erdtree Map]

How to get to Lamenter's Gaol in Elden Ring

You cannot reach Lamenter's Gaol from the Cerulean Coast or the Gravesite Plain. It is between both in elevation.
Here is one path you can take to reach it. It may not be the most direct path.

  • Start at the Grand Altar of Dragon Communion site of grace Map Link
  • Run south-west across the dead dragon until you see red flowers covering the ground, and jump down.
    • This will bring you to waypoint 1 in the map below.
  • Continue north-west across the red flowers. You should see the Charo's Hidden Grave site of grace, which is above the Cerulean Coast grace.
    • This brings you to waypoint 2 in the map.
  • Run through the pass into a swamp
    • A Death Rite Bird will spawn here, whether it is day or night, and start shooting projectiles at you.

  • Keep going north-west until you reach the wall bordering the starting zone.
    • This brings you to waypoint 3 in the map.
  • Continue hugging the wall as you move west, and you should find the cave containing Lamenter's Gaol
    • This is where the player is at in the map screenshot.

Lamenter's Gaol Walkthrough

ER Shadow of the Erdtree Lamenter's Gaol Video Walkthrough

The entrance to Lamenter's Gaol is just southeast of the Gravesite Plain site of grace on the map, and directly east of the Artist's Shack. Head northwest behind the huge dead dragon at the Grand Altar of Dragon Communion site of grace. Keep going above the Cerulean Coast and you'll eventually find the entrance to Lamenter's Gaol. Look for fences and metal cages outside the cave entrance. At the Lamenter's Gaol Site of Grace See Elden Ring Map, keep following the path going southeast until you arrive near a locked gate. Before the locked gate, there will be an opening on the left side of the wall. Go through the path on your left, then keep following the path.

You'll find two rooms with an open gate on the right side as you are traversing the path. The first room will only contain a Putrid Corpse. In the second room, you will encounter two Putrid Corpses. After dealing with the enemies in this room, open the chest to receive x1 Gaol Upper Level Key, which will be used to open the locked gate that you encountered earlier. Return to the path then keep following it until you cross a bridge. Enter the room across the bridge, in which you will encounter a Living Jar along with a Putrid Corpse. The room will be filled with numerous cages. On the northern side of the room, you'll find another room with three cages. On a dead body by the middle cage, you can pick up x1 Prattling Pate Lamentation.

Gaol Upper Level

Open the locked gate to get inside a room with a ladder and x1 Frozen Maggot that you can pick up. Climb down the ladder, and you will arrive in an area covered in ice. Living Jars will be running around the place, which you can easily deal with as they die in a few hits. You can also pick up numerous Frozen Maggots in the area, as they are scattered all around the area.

There are four rooms in the area that you can enter. In the room closest to the entrance, on the eastern side, you can pick up x1 Chilling Perfume Bottle. The second room will be the initially closed, but you can open the gate without any key. The room will contain a ladder going down, which will be useful later on. The third room will be the only room that is located a bit higher than the other rooms, requiring you to make a few small jumps. Lastly, in the northernmost room, you will find Living Jars around the room along with a locked gate on the northwestern corner of the room. There will also be a hole near the entrance that leads to loot.

Loot Detour and Navigating the Place

Near the entrance of the third room, you will encounter a Living Jar along with a Putrid Corpse patrolling the nearby room. Head inside the area that seems to be a jail area, onto another room on the eastern side. The cells couldn't be opened, so keep heading east. In the second room, the floor will collapse, making you fall into an area in which you will be surrounded by Putrid Corpses.

Go through the doorway, then keep following the path. You'll see a Putrid Corpse sitting on a cliff along the way. Head up the stairs to proceed further into Lamenter's Gaol. In the middle of the stairs, there will be a room on its landing. Enter the room then keep following its path, leading you to a room containing two Putrid Corpses and a dead body that you can loot for x1 Call of Tibia. Head back out the room, then continue walking up the stairs. There will be another landing with a Putrid Corpse hiding behind the corner. A room will be on the northern side of the landing. The room leads to an area overlooking the room filled with Living Jars.

Retrieving the Gaol Lower Level Key

Go down the slope, then defeat all the Living Jars in the area to freely explore it. On a structure made out of piled-up remains, you can pick up a Hefty Cracked Pot on top of it in the middle of small Living Jars holding hands. You can follow a path going southwest of the room, revealing just how big the whole area is. You can pick up x6 Living Jar Shard from a body on the southwestern corner of the area. A chest will be in the middle of the southwestern section part of the room, which you can acquire the Gaol Lower Level Key from when opening it. After acquiring the key, return to the stairs, then keep going up.

After obtaining the Gaol Lower Level Key, return to the room where you got the Call of Tibia from, but do not enter it. On the southern wall near the doorway to the room is an illusory wall that leads to a locked gate. Use the Gaol Lower Level Key on the locked gate, then enter the room. In this room, you can find a Shadow Realm Rune [4] and the Lamenting Visage torch. Head out the room by following the path going north.

When you get to the top of the stairs, enter the room. A Living Jar is hiding on the left side which can attack you by surprise if you aren't aware of it. Keep following the path, going through the doorway on the west side. If you jump down to the room, it will lead you back to the northernmost room. Head out of the room, then keep exploring the area containing many rooms if you haven't done that.

More Loot Detour

If you want to clear the area, jump down the hole near the entrance of the frozen room. After jumping, you'll find x2 Innard Meat from a dead body nearby. Follow the path going west. You will arrive in a dark area containing two Giant Rats. It's a dark area, so it is advisable to use a torch for this part. After dealing with the rats, follow the path going west, heading deeper into Lamenter's Gaol. You will encounter more Giant Rats in this area, which you deal with easily. In this room, there will be two paths that you can take. The path on the right will have a ladder going up, but two giant rats will be in the area. The ladder going up will lead you back to the frozen area with many rooms. The left path heads deeper into Lamenter's Gaol, leading you into a room with Giant Rats. You can also pick up the Clarifying Horn Charm +2 from a body near the blocked off path. The area being blocked is the one where you retrieved the Gaol Lower Level Key earlier.

To make your way back, go back to the tunnel where you came from. The path will then fork, a left and a right path. The right path leads to where you came from. The left path will be the one that leads back to the frozen room, as it leads you back to the ladder room you found earlier.

Facing The Lamenter

Make your way back to the frozen room by following the path, then head to the northernmost room filled with jars. You can use the Gaol Lower Level Key to open the locked door. Once you have unlocked the door, go down the ladder. You will see a boss fog directly in front of you. Prepare yourself for combat, as you will be facing the boss of this area, the Lamenter.

The Lamenter is a boss that is smaller than the usual bosses you encounter in this game. The Lamenter is an agile boss that relies on the use of its clones to deceive and confuse the player. Its clones will die in one hit, but hitting the clones will not damage the real Lamenter. Here is an in-depth guide for defeating the Lamenter.

After defeating the Lamenter, you will receive the Lamenter's Mask helm. The exit will be located on the southeastern side of the room.

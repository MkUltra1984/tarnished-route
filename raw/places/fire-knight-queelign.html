Fire Knight Queelign is a Spirit and Summon in Elden Ring. It is a brand-new Spirit in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. Fire Knight Queelign uses a greatsword that resembles a spear, and can also perform fire spells.

  • For more information about this NPC, please visit the NPC page of Fire Knight Queelign (NPC).

Ashen remains in which spirits yet dwell.
Use to summon the spirit of Fire Knight Queelign.
Attacks immediately after being summoned.

Spirit of a knight granted relief by the Iris of Grace.
Wields a spear-like greatsword that impales foes upon fire, keeping the taint of impurity away from fair mother Marika.

That grace was his salvation, though it was but a fleeting blessing. And having been saved, all that was left to him was to wage war.


Where to find Fire Knight Queelign

Where to Find Fire Knight Queelign

  • Give Fire Knight Queelignthe Iris of Grace.
  • The NPC is located in the Prayer Room of the Church District in Shadow Keep. See Shadow of the Erdtree Map

Elden Ring Fire Knight Queelign Guide

  • Fire Knight Queelign is exclusive to Shadow of the Erdtree, and cannot be obtained in the base game.
  • Upgrades with Ghost Gloveworts.
  • Cost: 123 FP.
  • NOTE: Patch 1.13 Increased Vigor and Faith.

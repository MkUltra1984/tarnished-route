Scadutree Fragment is an Upgrade Material in Elden Ring, debuting in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. Scadutree Fragments are optional consumables that can be used to simultaneously enhance offensive and defensive capabilities at any Sites of Grace, acting like a permanent boost to your damage dealt and damage negation in the Land of Shadow.

With the calibration update 1.12.2 attack and damage negation curve scaling of the Scadutree Blessing have been revised. The attack and damage negation has been increased for the first half of the maximum amount of Blessing enhancements, and the second half will be more gradual. The attack and damage negation granted by the final level of Blessing enhancements has been slightly increased.

  • For the other part of the blessing, check the Revered Spirit Ash page.

A splinter of the Scadutree that contains golden brilliance. Acquired at the Church of Marika and other places in the realm of shadow. Consume these at sites of grace to bolster your Scadutree Blessing. The Scadutree Blessing bolsters the bearer's ability to deal and negate damage but has no influence outside the realm of shadow. It is said that when the Scadutree crumbles from its core that it will scatter across the entire realm of shadow. The people of the Erdtree collect the splinters and use them in prayer.

Elden Ring Scadutree Fragment Guide

As you journey through the Realm of Shadow, you'll come across Scadutree Fragments. After finding one, rest at any Site of Grace to access the new Shadow Realm Blessing tab. In this menu, you can spend your Scadutree Fragments to level up your character in a unique way. Enhancing your Scadutree Blessing increases the damage you deal and reduces the damage you take. However, the cost in Scadutree Fragments rises with each level. Keep in mind that your Scadutree Blessing only applies while inside the Realm of Shadow and has no effect in the Lands Between (the base game).

Although this mechanic is optional, enemies and bosses in the Realm of Shadow are extremely challenging without them. If you're struggling with damage output or survivability, consider exploring to level up your Scadutree Blessing before facing more bosses. You don't need to max out your Scadutree Blessing; while it continues to increase your stats, the first 12 levels provide the most significant boost, with diminishing returns afterward. You can review the exact effects of each Scadutree Blessing level below.

Early Scadutree Fragments Guide

You can find all of these items in our Shadow of the Erdtree Map, however there are 10 Scadutree Fragments you easily acquire in the first region before facing a single Boss. This guide will get you to Scadutree Blessing level 5 with one additional fragment left over. If you need more, there is a second part to this guide that details how to get into the next region early and get to Scadutree Blessing level 10.

  • x1 East of Scorched Ruins Site of Grace: Dropped by a Shadowpot Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x2 Church of Consolation: From Gravesite Plain Site of Grace, head east and follow the path to the church. Mind the Black Knight that patrols within the church grounds. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x1 Three-Path Cross Site of Grace: Found next to a Miquella's Cross. This is where you meet Redmane Freyja and Hornsent. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x1 Main Gate Cross Site of Grace: Follow the main path from Three-Path Cross, through the tunnel and up some stone stairs, to find another fragment near a Miquella's Cross. This is where you meet Sir Ansbach and Moore. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x1 west of Prospect Town: Dropped by a Shadowpot. Head southeast from the Main Gate Cross along the cliffside to the Cliffroad Terminus Site of Grace. From there, go west to a graveyard and defeat the enemy. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x1 at a Marika Statue: Look north from the Three-Path Cross to find the Ellac Greatbridge. Cross the bridge, avoiding the ballistae and soldiers to reach a camp with a Marika Statue. The fragment is next to it. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x1 inside Castle Ensis: From the Castle Front Site of Grace, pass the troll guarding the staircase and follow the main path. There is a Miquella's Cross behind a Black Knight. Grab the fragment and touch the Site of Grace before the Black Knight reaches you. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x1 Pillar Path Cross Site of Grace: Head southeast from the Castle Front Site of Grace and follow the left path. Turn left at the fork to find another fragment near a Miquella's Cross. This is where you meet Thiollier. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link
  • x1 Belurat, Tower Settlement: Progress through the legacy dungeon to the Small Private Altar Site of Grace. Head upstairs, wait for the Horned Warrior to pass, then go down and jump onto the roof. Navigate the alleyway, climb the ladder, and head left. Avoid the scorpions, jump up the rubble, and go through the doorway. Beware of a Man-Fly ambush as you enter. A Miquella's Cross is at the end of this room along with a Scadutree Fragment. Shadow of the Erdtree Map Link

This next section involves getting into the next region early. If you do not want to explore past Gravesite Plain at this moment, you can stop reading until you reach this region through normal boss progression.

From the Castle Front site of grace, run south until you see the Ruined Forge Lava Intake. There is a path leading downwards to the left of this dungeon, follow it and it will take you to a poison swamp. Traverse the swamp and you will find a sealed spirit spring to the east, going past the Elder's Hovel on the way. There is a pile of rocks nearby that you have to break before jumping up. After doing so, jump up the second spirit spring, but aim directly northeast while doing so to land outside the fort (alternatively, land on the fort and activate the Back of Fort of Reprimand Grace, then fight your way out). Run around the fort to the front entrance and go through the cave to the north. This path will take you to Scadu Altus where you can collect some more easy fragments. Head north past the Ghostflame Dragon to find Moorth Ruins for the next few fragments.

  • 1x at Moorth Ruins: Right next to the Miquella Cross. This is also where you meet Dryleaf Dane.
  • 1x west of Moorth Ruins: From the Moorth Ruins site of grace, head west to find an enemy encampment. Inside is a Marika Statue with the fragment.
  • 1x northeast of Moorth Ruins: From the Moorth Ruins site of grace, head northeast to find a river. Run through to find another Marika Statue at the entrance to a cave.
  • 1x south of Moorth Ruins: From the Moorth Ruins site of grace, head southeast to find a shadow enemy with a shining pot near the ravine. Kill it to get the fragment, but watch out for the Curseblade that will catch you as you approach.
  • 1x at Scaduview Cross: From the Moorth Highway, South site of grace, run through the cave that leads to the Ghostflame Dragon, then head northeast to find a sealed spirit spring. Activate it and jump up. Run south after getting up the cliff and turn east to find another Miquella Cross.
  • 1x at Highroad Cross: From the Moorth Ruins site of grace, head northwest and follow the path until you get to an intersection. Turn south and you will see a Miquella Cross with the fragment. This is where you meet Needle Knight Leda proper in the Realm of Shadow.
  • 2x at Church of the Crusade: From the Highroad Cross site of grace, turn northwest and head towards the church. You will be invaded by Fire Knight Queelign upon entering the church, but you can pick up the fragments next to the Marika Statue before he gets a chance to kill you.
  • 1x at an enemy encampment: From the Highroad Cross site of grace, you should see the Furnace Golem to the north. Head towards it, and grab the fragment from a Marika Statue near the entrance.
  • 1x at Temple Town Ruins: You can reach the ruins by going behind the Marika Statue in front of the cave northeast of Moorth Ruins. Traverse the poison swamp and then run southwest from the Ancient Ruin Base site of grace. Circle around the ruins until you find a very small hole in the wall. Go up the stairs and jump into the room covered in the red tarp to find the fragment.

You should have collected 20 Scadutree Fragments if you followed the guide fully. This gets you up to Scadutree Blessing level 10, which should be more than enough for the first few bosses. You can find a list of all Scadutree Fragments at the bottom of this page.

Elden Ring all Scadutree Fragment Locations

Where to Find All Scadutree Fragments: Scadutree Fragment in the new Shadow of the Erdtree DLC is somewhat similar to Golden Seeds or Sacred Tears. It can be found in key locations and is scattered everywhere, often off the beaten path. Exploring off the beaten path is crucial to fully maximizing your Shadow Realm Blessing and increasing your chances of successfully completing the DLC. For example, if you find an encampment of knights with a mini-boss, there is likely to be a Scadutree Fragment not too far away. Therefore, diligently exploring and venturing off the main paths will be essential to uncovering these blessings and optimizing your gameplay experience. There are 50 Scadutree Fragments found in Elden Ring Shadow of the Erdtree.

Altus Plateau

Region


Map Locations NPCs Items Enemies & Bosses

Altus Plateau is a Location in Elden Ring. The Altus Plateau region can be accessed either by:

  • Using the Grand Lift of Dectus. (Requires the collection of both halves of the Dectus Medallion, which can be done at an early stage in the game).
  • Going through the Ruin-Strewn Precipice (after making your way through the Ravine-veiled Village, the Miner's Caves and Ruin-Strewn Precipice and defeating Magma Wyrm Makar).
  • A third, "secret" route can be utilized to reach the Altus Plateau region by being intentionally swallowed and killed by the Abductor Virgin at the bottom of the Raya Lucaria waterwheel. Doing so will take you to an otherwise secluded area inside Volcano Manor. From here you can reach the Altus Plateau by defeating the double Abductor Virgins boss. (Please note that this is might be a difficult boss fight for low-leveled characters, but if you fail to defeat them you can teleport out as soon as you have unlocked the site of grace).
  • A fourth, more complicated (and even more difficult) path to Dectus is through Nokron and Deeproot Depths. This involves activating the Radahn Festival, defeating Starscourge Radahn, making your way through Nokron and defeating the twin Valiant Gargoyles, traveling through the Deeproot Depths, and finally defeating a quintet of NPC human summons as part of Fia's questline.
    This will allow you to teleport directly into Leyndell, the Royal Capital, from which you can make your way over the North-Eastern bridge over the moat. The gate at the end of this bridge is blocked by a Boss Fog Gate that cannot be passed until the Draconic Tree Sentinel on the other side has been vanquished, but you can parkour jump with torrent on to the cliff on the West-side of the gate.

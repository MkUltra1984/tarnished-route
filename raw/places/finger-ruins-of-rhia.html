Finger Ruins of Rhia

Region

Large stone fingers erect at the basin in the Southern Shore region.

Map Locations NPCs Items Enemies & Bosses

Finger Ruins of Rhia is a Region/Location in Elden Ring, added with the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. The Finger Ruins of Rhia is found in the Southern Shore region. You can reach this location by heading south from Cerulean Coast site of grace. When you are about to reach Cerulean Coast Cross, turn to northeast direction and travel along the coastline until you reach a sloped coffin ship. At this point, proceed north and the Finger Ruins of Rhia site of grace can be found on edge of a nearby cliff. See Shadow of the Erdtree Map

Finger Ruins of Rhia Map

Finger Ruins of Rhia is revealed with Map: Scadu Altus

  • The Finger Ruins of Rhia is located in the Cerulean Coast region. To get there, begin at the Pillar Path Cross Site of Grace See Elden Ring Map directly south of Castle Ensis. Head north of the Site of Grace, wherein you will follow the cliff until you see a part of it that you can jump on. Make your way to the bottom using the rocks, then head south to a cave opening leading to the Ellac River Cave Site of Grace See Elden Ring Map. Keep heading south until you get to the waterfalls, then carefully jump across the rock formations. Head south, and you can activate the Ellac River Downstream Site of Grace See Elden Ring Map. From here continue traveling until you see glowing blue flowers around the area, which means that you are in the Cerulean Coast.

From the Cerulean Coast Cross - Site of Grace, head directly north past a few large tombstones and look over the cliff edge to find a path leading east to the beach below. Drop down and follow the path to the shore. Continue past the Fingercreepers and follow the shoreline northeast. Continue east until you reach another ramp-like structure. This time, proceed up the ramp and follow the path to the right, leading to the Ruins of Rhia - Site of Grace See it in the Elden Ring Map.

(See our Shadow of the Erdtree Game Progress Route page for more info.)

Finger Ruins of Rhia Walkthrough

Meet Count Ymir at the Cathedral of Manus Metyr and speak to him, he will give you the Ruins Map and the Hole-Laden Necklace. Follow his instructions with the map and head to Finger Ruin of Rhia.

Fast travel to the Finger Ruins of Rhia Site of Grace and make your way down, be careful of the Fingercreepers and Lamprey
 there. Once you reach the bell, interact with it and you will be granted the Crimson Seed Talisman +1.

Return to the Cathedral of Manus Metyr and speak with Count Ymir again and he will grant you the Ruins Map (2nd) and Beloved Stardust. It is time to head to Finger Ruins of Dheo for the next bell.

Finger Ruins of Rhia Lore

Elden Ring Finger Ruins of Rhia Gallery and Notes

  • You can get to the Southern Shore from the Gravesite Plain following the Ellac River downstream. Cross the Ellac Greatbridge, then head down the slope to the right and stick to the left wall until you reach Ellac River Cave. From here, you can follow the river downstream to reach Cerulean Coast.
  • The plateau on top of the large ramp leading into the Ruins has a Remembrance Duplication Coffin that can be used to duplicate Remembrances in the same manner as a Walking Mausoleum. Elden Ring Map Link

Legacy Dungeons | Elden Ring Wiki

This page was last edited on 5 August 2026, at 18:34.
From Elden Ring Wiki


Legacy Dungeons in Elden Ring are special fortresses and areas with significant challenge and exploration rewards. Legacy Dungeons are home to Lords, and players will access them to challenge the Bosses that dwell within and obtain pieces of the Elden Ring. You cannot ride Torrent within these locations.

  • See Locations for walkthroughs for larger open-world areas
  • See Minor Dungeons for Caves, Catacombs, etc
  • See Walkthrough and Game Progress Route for progression help
  • See Elden Ring Map and Maps for detailed maps of all areas

List of Legacy Dungeons in Elden Ring

Elden Ring Base Game Dungeons

These are the main Legacy Dungeons in the base game of Elden Ring:

  1. Stormveil Castle
  2. Raya Lucaria Academy
  3. Volcano Manor
  4. Leyndell, Royal Capital
  5. Crumbling Farum Azula
  6. Miquella's Haligtree / Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree

The following areas are still structured like Legacy Dungeons, though they are much smaller in size:

  1. Redmane Castle
  2. Leyndell, Ashen Capital
  3. Castle Morne
  4. Caria Manor
  5. Shaded Castle
  6. Castle Sol
  7. Ruin-Strewn Precipice
  8. Subterranean Shunning-Grounds
  9. Ainsel River
  10. Lake of Rot

And finally, there's a certain number of separate areas that are very much like Legacy Dungeons despite allowing you to ride Torrent within them:

  1. Siofra River
  2. Nokron, Eternal City
  3. Mohgwyn Palace
  4. Deeproot Depths

Elden Ring Shadow of the Erdtree Dungeons

Shadow of the Erdtree main Legacy Dungeons in Elden Ring:

  1. Belurat Tower Settlement
  2. Shadow Keep
  3. Enir-Ilim

Shadow of the Erdtree minor Legacy Dungeons in Elden Ring:

  • Castle Ensis
  • Fort of Reprimand
  • Midra's Manse

Limgrave

Region

Limgrave is a lush, expansive section of the Tenebrae Demesne. Golden trees and tall grass and bushes provide plenty of sustenance for the local wildlife, that features boars, sheep, goat and rodents in addition to flying creatures such as eagles and owls. More sinister and aggressive wildlife also exists, and those venturing forth should be prepared to combat them.

Map Locations NPCs Items Enemies & Bosses

Limgrave is a Location and Region in Elden Ring. The Limgrave region is the first accessible area of the game. Players will emerge from one of the underground catacombs and be treated to Limgrave's lush environments and scenery.

Limgrave is a lush, expansive section of the Tenebrae Demesne. Golden trees and tall grass and bushes provide plenty of sustenance for the local wildlife, that features boars, sheep, goat and rodents in addition to flying creatures such as eagles and owls. More sinister and aggressive wildlife also exists, and those venturing forth should be prepared to combat them.

Limgrave Map & Map Fragments in Elden Ring

  • Fragment West Limgrave: Map (Limgrave, West) is found at a pillar in Gatefront Ruins, just south from the Gatefront Site of Grace. Elden Ring Map Link
  • Fragment East Limgrave: Map (Limgrave, East) is found North-East of Mistwood Ruins, in front of a pillar containing what appears to be ancient writings. Elden Ring Map Link

Waygates

  • Divine Bridge Waygate':' Found at the edge of the first broken segment of the Divine Bridge east of Stormveil Castle. Transports you to the Divine Tower of Limgrave. Elden Ring Map Link
  • Sending Gate (Bestial Sanctum): Found at the edge of the shallows northeast of the Third Church of Marika. Transports you to the Bestial Sanctum in Caelid. Elden Ring Map Link
  • Teleporter Chest (Sellia Crystal Tunnel): Found in a cellar at the Dragon-Burnt Ruins. Transports you to the Sellia Crystal Tunnel in Caelid. Elden Ring Map Link

  • Enia - found at the northern collapsed bridge at Stormhill next to Stormveil Castle.

  • D Hunter of the Dead - can be found west of Summonwater Village - Elden Ring Map Link

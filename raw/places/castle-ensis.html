Castle Ensis

Legacy Dungeon
Location

Gravesite Plain

Recommended Levels

150 - Above

Bosses

Rellana, Twin Moon Knight

Castle Ensis is a Minor Legacy Dungeon in Elden Ring. Castle Ensis is a dungeon exclusive to the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. Castle Ensis can be located north-northeast of the Three Path Cross checkpoint (site of grace). Castle Ensis is a small castle where players can encounter scholars of magic, deadly hounds, noblemen capable of using magical attacks, and soldiers of Messmer.

Full Castle Ensis Walkthrough

Click here to go to the Speedrun Walkthrough.

The Entrance to Castle Ensis

As you enter the Castle Ensis area, you will encounter a bridge. Underneath the left side of the bridge that you are on is a path to a room that has big barrells along with a body that you can loot. Get the Spelldrake Talisman +3 from the body, then head back to bridge.

Once you cross the bridge, there is a body on the left side of the bridge that contains x3 Black Pyrefly. Across the bridge, you will encounter a Troll Knight guarding the area. Be ready for combat, as this can be a tough enemy to defeat. After killing the Troll Knight, you will see an entrance to a room on the right. Once you enter it, you will encounter a Lazuli Sorcerer. Once you have dealt with the enemy, there will be a body on the immediate left corner of the room containing a Shadow Realm Rune [4]. Behind the building of the room is a ramp with a body on it that has x1 Somber Smithing Stone [3] when picked up.

Once you have cleared the building, follow the path opposite of the building leading to a stairway heading deeper into the castle. On the western side by the cliff before the walls, you can loot a dead body that has x1 Somber Smithing Stone [1]. Then, in the middle of the stairs, you can climb up a cliff with a dead body that has x1 Broken Rune on it. Keep following the stairs going up, and you will encounter an Exile Soldier along with a Rotten Stray to your right, and another of the same set of enemies on the left side. Head up the stairs that the Exile Soldier and Rotten Stray were guarding on the left side, and you'll find yourself in an area with a ladder. Climb up the ladder to find a chest containing the Milady See Elden Ring Map, a light greatsword.

Loot Detour

Find your way back down the ladder and the stairs to a small cavern directly facing the stairs. Keep following the cavern, and you will find a body containing an item that you cannot reach yet. Once you exit the small cavern, you will see a body in front of you that contains x2 Flight Pinion. Follow the first ramp heading up, and you will see two Messmer Foot Soldiers gathering around a bonfire and a Lesser Black Knight nearby, so be prepared to engage them in combat. After dealing with the enemies, if you look down by the cliff before the bonfire area, you can drop safely into an area below. You will find remains resting right on the ledge containing x10 Bolt. Behind the remains will be the cavern that you went through earlier, and you will get to loot the body you saw earlier, containing x3 Glintslab Firefly.

Find the Castle Ensis Checkpoint Site of Grace

Backtrack your way to the ramps. You'll encounter a Black Knight near the first ramp. On the first ramp, you'll find a room to the left that has a chest containing the Loyal Knight's Cookbook See Elden Ring Map. Head back outside, then go up the second ramp. In the middle of the second ramp, you'll find a pathway leading to a body with an item. A Rotten Stray is hiding around the corner, so be careful as you approach to loot the body. After dealing with the Rotten Stray, loot the body to receive a Silver-Pickled Fowl Foot. Go back to the ramp and keep following it. At the top of the ramp, you will find a closed gate. Look over to the left of the gate, and you will pick up x1 Castle Cross Message. You will also find a cross, and 1x Scadutree Fragment. Near the Scadutree Fragment drop, you will find the Castle Ensis Checkpoint Site of Grace See Elden Ring Map. Northwest of the Site of Grace, you can pick up a Somber Smithing Stone [1] on a corpse sitting against the wall.

Finding A Way Inside

On the right side of the locked gate, there will be an Messmer Soldier looking towards an area below. Dispose of the soldier, then jump down the area. You'll find a body immediately after the first jump which contains x3 Knot Resin. You'll notice a hole leading to a cavern in the ground. You can jump safely below, and you will deal with three Rotten Strays immediately after dropping down. You can loot a body for x10 Arrow beside where you dropped. Follow the path of the cavern. You will find yourself on a cliffside path. The path to your right leads to the rooftop of the structure that you looted near the Troll Knight. Be careful as you're traversing the path, as there is a small gap that you need to jump right before the rooftop. As you get to the rooftop, you will find an eagle in front of a body on the other end. Loot the body to find x1 Starlight Shards.

Head back to the junction of the cliffside path, then follow the left path leading to a waterfall. Jump on the rocks of the waterfall to get on top of it. You will see two turtles on the left side, and an archway leading to an area with a ladder on the left side leading to a room and a cavern behind a waterfall. There is a dead body on the left side before the cavern entrance which contains x3 Drawstring Royal Magic Grease. At the end of the cavern, you will find a dead body which you can loot for x4 Knot Resin. Head back outside the cavern, then climb up the ladder. As you enter the room, you'll encounter two Messmer Soldiers. Deal with the enemies, then head through the doorway on the far-left corner of the room.

The area outside will be the locked gate you encountered earlier, which you can open by pushing the lever inside the room directly across from where you came from. Head up the stairs, and you end up in a bridge with four Messmer Soldiers. The fourth soldier uses a heavy crossbow if you are not in melee-range, so be cautious of that. On the left side of the other end of the bridge, you can loot a body to receive x3 String. Follow the path leading south right after the bridge. Be careful here, as a there is a Rotten Stray hiding in the bushes on the right just as the path gets tighter. You will end up on a cliffside path.

Follow the path on the right, and you will face two Noble Sorcerers right before a staircase. There will also be a dead body on the left that you can loot for x2 Glintslab Firefly. You can easily deal with the first Noble Sorcerer as you can attack it easily. However, to deal with the second Noble Sorcerer, you can follow a path on the right of the stairs that leads to a ladder that you can climb to get on top of a fort. At the top of the fort, you will face a Page. You can pick up x1 Somber Smithing Stone [6] on a dead body just beside the Page. After dealing with the Page, you can then go to the cliff where the second Noble Sorcerer by jumping off of an opening of the fort. You can also access the area by jumping on the cliff from where the ladder is located.

Dealing with Moonrithyll, Carian Knight

After dealing with the enemies before the staircase, you can then proceed by going up the stairs. You will then end up right outside of a structure being guarded by an NPC Invader, Moonrithyll, Carian Knight. Moonrithyll uses attacks similar to the moveset of the colossal sword. He can use jumping attacks, thrusts, and wide attacks when fighting. You can easily dispose of Moonrithyll by staggering him, which can be easy if you're using a build that deals a lot of Poise damage. After defeating Moonrithyll, he will drop Moonrithyll's Knight Sword, a Colossal Sword.

In the first room, you will find two rooms connecting to it. The room on the west side is just another way inside the structure. The room on the south side is a chapel with a Spirit NPC praying to Queen Marika. On the podium of the chapel, you can pick up the talisman Rellana's Cameo.

After clearing the chapel, head to the hallway near the entrance of the chapel, in which you'll encounter a Lazuli Sorcerer. You can obtain x1 Shadow Realm Rune [2] from a dead body in the hallway. Across the dead body, there is a room that another Lazuli Sorcerer is hiding in. You can deal with it now so you will not be overwhelmed with enemies as you progress through the hallway. You will encounter another Lazuli Sorcerer right around the corner of the hallway just after the dead body. If you did not clear the previous room, the enemy will come out right as you fight the other Lazuli Sorcerer in the hallway.

Finding More Loot

After killing the Lazuli Sorcerers, continue following the hallway, which leads to an area outside. There will be a dead body ahead which you can loot for x1 Glintslab Firefly. A Noble Sorcerer will be attacking you on top of a structure. If you have ranged attacks, you can easily dispose of this enemy. At the landing of the stairs, there will be a gate on the south side that you can open. On the left immediate of the gate you opened, there will be an area below that you can jump down to. You can pick up x3 Dewgem from a dead body sitting by the cliff. You will find a ladder beside the dead body that you can climb to get on the rooftop of the structure that the Noble Sorcerer was attacking you from. You can dispose of the Page along with the Noble Sorcerer here in this area. On the western side of the rooftop, you can pick up x1 Somber Smithing Stone [2] off of a dead body. There is an area that you can jump down to, before the dead body on the west to find a corpse containing the Glintblade Trio sorcery. Jump down, then enter the room to your right. You can open a chest containing the Wolf Crest Shield See Elden Ring Map. Head up the ladder to return to the entrance of the building.

Backtrack your way to the gate that you opened, traverse the path. You will encounter an Exile Soldier that you can get a jump on as it is facing the other way. Continue following the path, and head down the stairs to fight three Messmer Soldiers along with two Rotten Strays. The other path, which is the one above the stairs leading down, also has an Exile Soldier that you can easily deal with. After dealing with the enemies, you can loot two dead bodies, one containing x1 Somber Smithing Stone [6] and x1 Somber Smithing Stone [5].

Continue following the path until you find a staircase on the side of a tower, in which you will encounter an Exile Soldier as you turn on the corner of the stairs. Once you arrive at the top of the stairs, you can find a chest in the room to your left containing the Imbued Sword Key. Across the bridge, you will encounter a Lesser Black Knight guarding the passage to a lift. After defeating the knight, ride the lift to go up the structure. After using the lift, you can follow the path on your right to discover the Castle-Lord's Chamber Site of Grace See Elden Ring Map. On the eastern side of the room, you can head to the balcony outside to loot a dead body containing a Somber Smithing Stone [4]. You can find an area that you can drop down to if you look by the railings of the balcony. You will find a tower near a graveyard, and on top of it, you can obtain Ash of War: Wing Stance inside a chest. Travel back to the Castle-Lord's Chamber Site of Grace. You can follow the stairs up to the west of the Site of Grace which leads to an entrance that can only be entered by going through the fog. Near the boss fog, you can touch the summon sign for Needle Knight Leda to help you during the boss fight. Make sure to do the necessary preparations that you need.

Fighting Rellana, Twin Moon Knight and finishing up

The boss that you will be fighting in this area is Rellana, Twin Moon Knight. She uses dual light greatswords that she can enchant with magic and fire. Her melee attacks consist of sweeping and slashing movement, which you should be patient in dealing with. She also uses a spell similar to the Glintblade Phalanx to attack you from afar. For an in-depth guide for defeating Rellana, you can go to this page for more information.

After you have defeated Rellana, Twin Moon Knight, you will receive the Remembrance of the Twin Moon Knight and you will unlock the Ensis Moongazing Grounds Site of Grace See Elden Ring Map. You can exchange the Remembrance of the Twin Moon Knight with Enia at the Roundtable Hold to receive Rellana's Twinblade and the sorcery Rellana's Twin Moons.

Loot Behind Castle Ensis

You can access a section behind Castle Ensis from the Ensis Moongazing Grounds by going through the archway on the northern side of the room. Follow the path but traverse it near the cliffside on the left. Drop down the cliff, then follow the stream going back to Castle Ensis. You will eventually arrive at a rampart in Castle Ensis in which you will find a chest. Open the chest, and you will receive the Carian Sorcery Sword.

Speed Run Walkthrough

Castle Ensis Entrance

Before you cross the bridge going to Castle Ensis, go under it to discover a room. In this room, you can find the Spelldrake Talisman +3 from a dead body. Head back up the bridge, then cross it. In the middle of the bridge, you can loot a corpse for x3 Black Pyrefly. At the end of the bridge, you must defeat a Troll Knight guarding the area. You will see a structure nearby, enter it and you'll encounter a Lazuli Sorcerer. Defeat the enemy, then loot the corpse inside the room for a Shadow Realm Rune [4]. You can head behind the room to find a corpse with a Somber Smithing Stone [3].

After clearing the building, follow the path leading to a staircase heading up. Before heading up the stairs, you can loot a corpse on a cliff on the west side for a Somber Smithing Stone [1]. Then, climb the stairs going up. In the middle of the stairs, you can find a cliff that you can get to, which you can loot a corpse for a Broken Rune. Keep following the stiars going up, and you will encounter a Messmer Soldier along with a Rotten Stray on the right side. On the left side, another Messmer Soldier and a Rotten Stray will be heading down the stairs. Defeat the enemies, then climb up the stairs to get on top of the wall structure. Climb up the ladder in the area, then open the chest to obtain the Milady light greatsword. Afterwards, head back down the stairs then follow the cavern opposite of the stairs.

At the end of the cavern, you will be near the entrance to Castle Ensis. Right at the exit of the cavern, you can loot a corpse for x2 Flight Pinion. Follow the ramp heading up, and you will find two Messmer Soldiers gathering around a fire. Defeat the enemies, then jump down the ledge near the area. You can find a corpse containing x10 Bolt here. Head inside the cavern, and you can loot a dead body hanging by a ledge for x3 Glintslab Firefly. Drop down, and you'll find yourself in the short cavern you were in earlier. Go back outside, then go up the first small ramp.

On the first level of the ramp, you can find the Loyal Knight's Cookbook inside a chest in a room on the left side. Head back outside, then go up the second ramp. You will encounter a Black Knight here, so be prepared for combat. After defeating the Black Knight, head up the second ramp. In the middle of the second ramp, you can find a small opening which leads to a corpse. By the corpse, a Rotten Stray will be waiting around the corner to attack you. Defeat the Rotten Stray, then loot the corpse for a Silver-Pickled Fowl Foot. Go up the third ramp, and you'll find a closed gate. On the left side, you can find a Scadutree Fragment and a Castle Cross Message. Nearby, you can activate the Castle Ensis Checkpoint Site of Grace. You can pick up a Somber Smithing Stone [1] on a corpse west of the Site of Grace.

Opening the Closed Gate

After activating the Site of Grace, you can find a Messmer Soldier looking over an area below. Defeat the Messmer Soldier, then drop down. After the first drop, you can loot a corpse for x3 Knot Resin. Drop down on the small opening, and you'll be surrounded by three Rotten Strays. Clear the area, then loot a dead body for x10 Arrow. Follow the cavern, and the path will fork. The right side will lead you to a rooftop with a corpse that you can loot for Starlight Shards. The left side will lead you deeper into Castle Ensis.

After clearing the right path, follow the path on the left side. Jump on the rocks of the waterfall. After arriving at the top of the waterfall, you'll find a ladder on the left side and a cavern on the right. Go to the right path, then loot the corpse by the entrance of the cavern to get x3 Drawstring Royal Magic Grease. Go inside the cavern, which will be short, and you can loot a corpse for x4 Knot Resin. Head outside the cavern, then go up the ladder. As you enter the room, two Messmer Soldiers will attack you. Defeat the soldiers, then head out the room through the doorway.

The area outside will lead you to the other side of the locked gate you found earlier. Go inside the room across, then push the lever to open the gate. After that, you can head up the stairs to get to a bridge with four Messmer Soldiers. The first three soldiers will use melee, while the one farthest from you will use a heavy crossbow. Clear the bridge, then you can loot a corpse near the bridge for x3 String. Follow the path going south, being careful of a Rotten Stray hiding behind the bush right as the path gets tighter. At the end, you will be on a cliffside path.

Follow the path on the right, and you will face two Noble Sorcerers. On the left, you can loot a corpse for x2 Glintslab Firefly. After defeating the two enemies, you can defeat the third sorcerer by going on the path just right before the staircase which leads to a ladder going up a rampart. Climb the ladder, then defeat the Page you will encounter. Loot a corpse nearby for a Somber Smithing Stone [6]. Afterwards, you can jump on the cliff to defeat the third Noble Sorcerer. Head down to where you came from, then go up the stairs.

At the top of the stairs, you'll be right outside of a structure guarded by an NPC Invader, Moonrithyll, Carian Knight. Moonrithyll drops the Moonrithyll's Knight Sword after defeating her. You can then head inside the chapel. Inside the chapel, you can find a Spirit NPC in front. In front of the spirit, you can pick up Rellana's Cameo. Afterwards, you can head to the hallway near the entrance to the chapel. A Lazuli Sorcerer will attack you as you walk through the hallway. You can obtain a Shadow Realm Rune [2] from a corpse along the hall. You can also find a room containing a Lazuli Sorcerer, which would be a good choice to defeat now so you won't be surrounded by enemies later.

Head out the room, then go around the corner. You will be attacked by another Lazuli Sorcerer hiding by the corner. After defeating the enemy, follow the hallway until you get outside of the structure. Be careful here, as a Noble Sorcerer will be attacking you from the top of a structure. Head up the stairs, picking up the Glintslab Firefly from a corpse on the way, then open the gate on the south side.

Loot Detour

Drop down on the ledge on the left side immediately after going through the gate. Pick up x3 Dewgem from a corpse by the cliff. Climb up the ladder beside the corpse to get to the roof of the structure that the Noble Sorcerer was attacking you from. Defeat the Noble Sorcerer and the Page so you can freely explore the area. On the west side of the rooftop, you can pick up a Somber Smithing Stone [2] off of a corpse. Then, you can drop down to an area on the side of the roof. You can find the Glintblade Trio sorcery right after dropping. Then, jump down to find a room that contains the Wolf Crest Shield. Head up the ladder to return to the entrance of the building. Make your way back to the gate you went through, then follow its path.

Castle Ensis Progress

As you follow the path, you will encounter a Messmer Soldier that you can backstab. Continue following the path until you go down a staircase. You will fight four Messmer Soldiers along with two Rotten Strays in this area. Afterwards, you can loot two corpses here for a Somber Smithing Stone [5] and Somber Smithing Stone [6]. Continue following the path, and you will see a staircase on the side of a tower. A Messmer Soldier will be in the middle of the stairs, so prepare yourself for combat. Afterwards, you can enter a room on the left. Open the chest to receive an Imbued Sword Key. Cross the bridge, then defeat the Black Knight guarding a passage. After defeating the Black Knight, you can head through the passageway to use the lift to go up.

Castle-Lord's Chamber

After arriving at the top of the lift, you will be in the Castle-Lord's Chamber. Activate the Site of Grace in this area, then head to the balcony outside. Loot the corpse for a Somber Smithing Stone [4], then drop down on the railings. Approach the tower by crossing the graveyard, and you can find the Ash of War: Wing Stance inside a chest at the top of the structure. Travel back to the Castle-Lord's Chamber Site of Grace, then prepare yourself for a boss fight.

Rellana, Twin Moon Knight

Once you are ready for the boss fight, head up the stairs near the Site of Grace. By the entrance to the boss room, you can touch a summon sign to summon Needle Knight Leda to assist you in defeating the boss. Enter the room, and you will be facing Rellana, Twin Moon Knight. For a comprehensive guide for the fight with Rellana, Twin Moon Knight, you may refer to this guide. After defeating Rellana, you will receive the Remembrance of the Twin Moon Knight which you can exchange for Rellana's Twinblade and the sorcery Rellana's Twin Moons.

Another Loot Detour

After defeating Rellana, you will unlock the Ensis Moongazing Grounds. Head north outside of Castle Ensis, then follow the path while hugging the cliff on the left side. Drop down the cliff when you're at a safe height, then follow the western bank of the stream back to Castle Ensis (heading south). You will see a rampart which you can get on. Explore the immediate area, and you can find a chest that you can open for the Carian Sorcery Sword.

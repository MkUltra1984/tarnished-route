Voiced By

Aimee-Ffion Edwards

Renna is an NPC in Elden Ring. Renna is a mysterious blue humanoid character distinguished by her four arms and a spectral second face overlapping her own. It is later revealed that "Renna" is merely an alias used by Ranni the Witch to conceal her true identity during her initial encounter with the Tarnished. Under this guise, she approaches the player early in their journey to bestow the ability to summon Spirit Ashes. These spirits come in different forms, including magic, Skeletons, Wolves, and many more. Renna will leave shortly after teaching you how to summon spirits and gifting you the necessary tools, setting the stage for her expansive questline later in the game.

"Unless of course, thou shouldst take the crown?"

Location/Where to Find Renna in Elden Ring

Renna can be found in the following location(s):

  • Found sitting by the ruins of the Church of Elleh in Limgrave. Return to the Church of Elleh via teleporting on the map menu, after acquiring the ability to summon your Spectral Steed from Melina to find her. Map Link
    • Manually going back to the Church (via walking there) won't make Renna appear. Sitting at a Site of Grace (any) will not work either.
    • Passing the time (done while resting at a Site of Grace), either inside or outside the church, will not make her appear.
    • Quitting out of the game and loading back in will not make her appear, regardless of time of the day.
    • Time of the day doesn't matter for making her appear. Once the player teleports to the Church of Elleh after acquiring the Spectral Steed Whistle, the time will automatically be set to night.
  • After talking to her and opting to tell her that you can summon Torrent, the Spectral Steed, she will gift the player with the Spirit Calling Bell key item, and the Lone Wolf Ashes.
  • Found again in Ranni's Rise where she reveals her true identity as Ranni the Witch. Here she begins a long and important quest relevant to much of the game's story.
  • If Melina appears to offer the player access to the Roundtable Hold, Ranni will no longer spawn at the Church even if you press cancel and don't actually go. You can still start her quest line at Ranni's Rise.
    • If you do not get the Spirit Calling Bell from Renna, you can buy the bell and the Lone Wolf Ashes from the Twin Maiden Husks in the Roundtable Hold.

Dialogue in Elden Ring: Renna

Introduction

  • This way, Tarnished. May I have a word.
  • A pleasure to meet thee, Tarnished. I am the witch Renna.
    'I'd heard tell of a Tarnished hurtling about atop a spectral steed.
    And upon looking into the matter, the talk, I surmise, is of thee.
    '    Thou'rt possessed of the power, no?
    To call forth the spectral steed named Torrent.

If you say you can call Torrent

  • Ah. As I had hoped. I was entrusted this, for thee. By Torrent's former master.
    'Tis a bell for calling forth spirits. Summon them with it, from ash unreturned to the Erdtree.
    '    The spirits will obey thine command but briefly, as they recall battles past.
    'Now it is thine. To do with as thou wishest.

Before disappearing

  • Forgive mine intrusion, Tarnished. I doubt we shall meet again. But all the same, learn well thee Lands Between.
    How long will it be, I wonder...Before the Tarnished tire of obeisance to the Two Fingers?

At Ranni's Rise

  • Oh, again we cross paths. I believe I said my name was Renna, when we last met. It pleaseth me to see Torrent hale and hearty...But Tarnished, what business hast thou here? I have no memory of inking thee an invitation.

No Particular Reason

  • No reason in particular, thou claimest? Intriguing. Then mayhaps fate hath steered thee to this reunion. Wilt thou enter into my service? I am the witch Ranni. I stole Death long ago, and search now for the dark path. That I might one day upend the whole of it, and rid the world of all that came before. Well? Has that roused thy interest?

Serve Ranni the Witch

  • I see. A choice well made. Thou'rt a rare sort. Not many would have accepted the offer. But I require as much of those under my command. I anticipate good work from thee. Good, then I ask we proceed with haste.
  • There is, in my service, a half-wolven warrior by the name of Blaidd. I would have thee join him in searching for the hidden treasure of Nokron, the Eternal City. I have called Blaidd to greet thee below. Take from him the particulars. Ah, and there wilt thou find Iji, my war counselor, and Seluvis, preceptor in the sorcerous arts, also. Heed not their peculiarities; feel secure in gaining from them what advantage thou canst. I am sure the others will be doing just the same.
  • Speak with the three who await thee below. Thou needst not indulge them unduly, but they too wish to appraise they worth. It hath been a passing long time since a newcomer entered my service, after all.

After Speaking to the Three

  • Ah, allow me to forewarn thee. I shall soon enter my slumber. And it will be some time before I wake. This doll's body is not without its hindrances...Still, I have high hopes for thee. I look forward to the good news when I arise.

Elden Ring Renna Notes and Trivia

  • The "Trivia" section on the IMDB page for Elden Ring claims that "Game of Thrones" actress Maisie Williams lends her voice to Renna character in the game's story trailer. However, there are no reliable sources that support this other than people claiming to recognise her voice in various online forums.

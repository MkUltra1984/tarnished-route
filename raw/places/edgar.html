Edgar (or Edgar the Revenger, Castellan Edgar) is an NPC in Elden Ring. Edgar is Irina's father and introduces himself as the warden of Castle Morne. He can be found sitting on a bench in a non-hostile area of the ramparts of the castle. His NPC Summon Sign can be used to fight against the Leonine Misbegotten once he has received Irina's Letter.

See Edgar Lore

I'm Edgar, warden of this castle as ordained by Lord Godrick himself. But you can see how things have turned out.

Edgar Location in Elden Ring

Edgar can be found at Castle Morne in the Weeping Peninsula. Map Link

  • To find Edgar:
    • Enter Castle Morne and go up the lift.
    • Go straight, past the Misbegotten on the mound, and turn right at the resting Pumpkin Head.
    • Climb the ladder at the end of the path on the right, up to the ramparts.
    • Follow the path of the ramparts. On the left, past a narrower section, where two flying Misbegotten are hovering, some boxes hide a ladder. Descend the ladder or simply jump down below to another section of the ramparts where some knights are fighting more Misbegotten.
    • Head straight toward the fight, past the gallows, and climb the set of stairs circling up to the next corner of the castle. Edgar will be sitting alone on a bench.
  • Edgar may move to the Bridge of Sacrifice, to Irina'slocation, as part of her questline. Map Link
  • This NPC can be fought
  • If defeated, drops:
    • Banished Knight's Halberd+8
    • 1000 Runes
    • Shabriri Grape (Whatever area he died in must be reloaded - the grape will appear where you usually find him in that area).
  • Even after completing Edgar's questline, he will invade your world when visiting the Revenger Shack in Liurnia. He will not invade if he is killed before Hyetta moves to Gate Town Bridge.

Edgar Questline: How to complete Edgar's Quest

Edgar's quest is part of Irina's questline. Speak to Irina near the first site of grace of Weeping Peninsula, and she will ask you to help deliver a letter, "Irina's Letter", to her father in Castle Morne, at the southern tip of the Weeping Peninsula.

  1. After successfully delivering her letter to Edgar, he will lament that he is not able to go to Irina because of his ongoing duties at Castle Morne - he cannot allow the Grafted Blade Greatsword to be stolen by the Misbegotten.
    • Upon delivering the letter to Edgar, return to Irina to find an Iron Cleaver embedded in the ground next to her dead body.
    • Killing Edgar will progress the questline in the same way.
  2. After defeating the Leonine Misbegotten and retrieving the sword, Edgar will thank the player and return to Irina.
  3. Edgarcan then be found next to Irina. Talk to him to progress the questline. If the player kills Edgar after talking to him at this location, you receive the Banished Knight's Halberd (+8), however he will not appear as a hostile invader at the revengers shack, and his Shabriri Grape can be obtained by travelling back to this location or reloading the area.
  4. If the player travels to the Revenger's Shack, Edgar will appear as a hostile invader in the form of Edgar the Revenger. If the player defeats him before killing the Leonine Misbegotten, then the player will miss out on Edgar as a summon, and he won't be in his first spot to thank the player and won't appear near Irina's corpse.

Dialogue in Elden Ring: Edgar

§Introduction

  • Ah, there's a face I've not seen before. I'm Edgar, warden of this castle as ordained by Lord Godrick himself. But you can see how things have turned out. The menials have all rebelled. They gave me good service, or so I thought, but it seems it was all an act. Foul creatures, as it's said. And true enough they're foul inside and out. I'm sorry to disappoint you, But whatever you've come here to do, I'm afraid Castle Morne won't hold much longer. Take this, by way of apology.

When attacked

  • Ugh! I haven't a clue what's got into you, but as Lord of this castle, I will stand for it no longer!
  • Ah, now I see! You had a hand in Irina's undoing! What could she have done to deserve that?
  • (On player kill) And now even your ilk have complicated matters.

Death

  • Please forgive me, Lord Godrick.
  • Forgive me, Irina... I will join you soon enough.

When giving Irina's Letter

  • I see, from Irina... Thank you. I'm in your debt. But I can't leave yet. Even if the castle should fall, as commander, I must remain. To ensure the treasured sword of Morne does not fall into the wrong hands.
  • If you see Irina, do tell her. That her father will come for her, once he's fulfilled his duty.

Upon Retrieving the Sword

  • "I'm in your debt. I'm no longer bound by duty. Once I've rescued Irina, I will spend my remaining days with her. Thanks to you, I will be reunited with Irina. And devote my remaining days to her. Irina has a gentle nature, though. I only hope it remains intact."

On Seeing Irina's Death

  • "Irina...How could this be...My daughter deserved better...The fault lies...with me. I chose duty over my daughter's safety. And that is how fate has answered...I'll find them. The foul wretches responsible for this. I'll hunt them down, and exterminate every last one of them. Rest assured, Irina. It will be done."

Elden Ring Edgar Notes & Trivia

  • Edgar wears an unobtainable variant of the the Banished Knight Set without a helmet. His unique unaltered Banished Knight Armor appears without ornamental horns.
  • During combat, Edgar uses a Banished Knight's Halberd (+8) with the Spinning Strikes Ash of War, and a sacred seal, which he uses to cast Golden Vow.

Edgar Lore

It would appear that Edgar has gone mad due to grief, suffered from losing his daughter, Irena. And it is believed that Hyetta is using Irena's Body somehow; no canon explanation is know thus far, although a few explainations are hypothesized in the wider lore community.
The gruesome implications of this information, is that the tarnished goes on to feed Hyetta her Fathers putrefied eyeball after brutally slaughtering him.

Lore Items Related to Edgar

Irina's Letter

Letter given by Irina, addressed to her father, the commander of Castle Morne.

Gorgeous silken handkerchief, lightly stained with blood. Words can be made out within.

Sacrificial Twig

A talisman fashioned from a dried twig, so slender that it might snap at the slightest touch.
Prevent rune loss upon death, but will be lost itself in exchange.

Believed to be a twig pruned from the Erdtree long, long ago.

Grafted Blade Greatsword

The storied sword of Castle Morne. A revenger's weapon, it is burdened with oceans of anger and regret.
One of the legendary armaments.

A lone surviving champion from a country now vanished was so determined to continue fighting that he claimed the swords of an entire clan of warriors.

Shabriri Grape

A yellowing, oozing eyeball of the infirm. The surface is shriveled, and the inside is squishy, not unlike a large, overly-ripe grape.

Give to the blind maiden to guide her to the distant light.

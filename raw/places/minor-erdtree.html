Minor Erdtree is an Incantation in Elden Ring. It was added with the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. Summons a small, illusory tree in front of the caster that restores HP in an area.


Secret incantation of Queen Marika.
Only the kindness of gold, without Order.

Creates a small, illusory Erdtree that continuously restores the HP of nearby allies.

Marika bathed the village of her home in gold,
knowing full well that there was no one to heal.


Where to find Minor Erdtree

Where to find Minor Erdtree:

  • Located at the Shaman Village in the Scaduview region. Found in the middle of the glowing field of flowers. Map Link

Elden Ring Minor Erdtree Guide

  • Erdtree Incantation
  • Stamina Cost: 30
  • Works similar to Warming Stones, healing self and allies in a fixed small area.
    • However, this incantation does not heal close enemies, unlike the Stones.
  • Minor Erdtree heals 9 HP every 0.25 seconds for 30 seconds, for a total of 1080 HP (unaffected by Old Lord's Talisman).
    • This can be seen in the Status screen as 18 HP every 0.5 seconds due to UI update rate limit.
    • Damages Wraiths and Revenants by (3% Max HP) + 10 HP every 1 second, and does not trigger Stance break.
    • NOTE: Casting DOES draw enemy attention.
    • Has a 0.3 meter radius that gradually expands to a maximum 3 meter radius over 2 seconds.
  • Stacks with Crimsonburst Crystal Tear, Warming Stones or Sunwarmth Stones and other healing incantations.
  • Melina summons a similar, bigger tree when fighting Morgott, she summons it once she dies.

NOTE: Patch 1.13: The healing has been changed, from 24 HP/s to 9 HP/0.25s.

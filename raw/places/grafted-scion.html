Grafted Scion is a Field Boss in Elden Ring. Grafted Scion is an amorphous mass of bodies that wields two swords and a greatshield, found in various locales, most prominently in Stormveil Castle. This is an optional boss as players don't need to defeat it to advance in the game.

Grafted Scion Combat information

  • Stance: 80
  • Parryable: Yes
  • Guard Break: Yes
  • Is vulnerable to a critical hit after being stance broken or parried
  • Weak spot: Head
  • Can block upcoming ranged attacks with its shield.
  • Damage: Standard, Strike, Pierce, Holy (1st)
  • Only first Grafted Scion has boss bars, but all don't respawn when killed

Negations (or Absorptions)

  • Standard: 0
  • Slash: -10
  • Strike: 0
  • Pierce: 0
  • Magic: 20
  • Fire: 20
  • Lightning: 20
  • Holy: 20
The negation numbers are the % of your damage that gets blocked. For example, if a negation is 60, 40% of that damage by that type will go through and 60% will be negated. Bigger number = less damage. A negation of 100 means no damage goes through, and a negation of -100 mean the enemy takes 2x damage from that source. 0 means damage goes pretty much as is.

Elden Ring Grafted Scion Boss Guide

Grafted Scion Fight Strategy

The Best Tips for Grafted Scion:
  • Avoid guarding as much as possible, focus on rolling instead.
  • Use quick to cast spells like Glintstone Pebble.
  • Vagabound and Samurai classes are the best to kill him during 1st encounter due to their stance-breaking skills.
Melee Strategy
Bait: Keep your distance and try to get it to use Leaping Stab, Crawling Stabs or (later in the fight) Piercing Stabs. Dodge the attack, get in a couple hits, and then back off. Don't get greedy: it will often use Scream after being damaged, and you don't want to be nearby for that. You can also watch for two consecutive clockwise slashes: this always signals the end of a combo, and you can safely get in a hit after it as well.
Magic and Ranged Strategy
Bide Your Time: Keep your distance and focus on dodging if you can and guarding if you have to. Don't attack until you know it's safe, such as during the long recovery period after Leaping Stab, Crawling Stabs, its double-sword Stab, or (later in the fight) Piercing Stabs. It has long combos and can react to being shot with moves that will close distance, so stay on your toes once it has recovered.

Grafted Scion Notes & Other Trivia

  • Most player's first encounter with this Spider Boss known as the Grafted Scion will not usually end in its defeat especially since this occurs at the very beginning of the game. This encounter is part of the tutorial process and although you must face this Boss to progress, it is not required that you defeat it at this time. In fact, it is not expected that you have a successful initial encounter since this area can be returned to at a later time in the game which some players may not find out until later on. Complete this encounter with either a win or defeat and continue about your game until you reach The Four Belfries.
  • If you didn't defeat Grafted Scion during the tutorial, you can return and face it again through the waygate marked "Precipice of Anticipation" in The Four Belfries. This can be done after you have returned with more experience, improve your equipment and level. This is especially beneficial for people who want to receive its unique drops: Ornamental Straight Sword and Golden Beast Crest Shield. Players can also face the same type of Spider boss at the Fringefolk Hero's Grave, Stormveil Castle and Liurnia of the Lakes. See the Location guides above on how to recognize the Boss arena and how to get there.
  • Use the strategy guides and keep your distance against this Boss while wearing out its hp eventually leading to its defeat. Use a combination of dodging and waiting for attack windows before closing in for your attack. Once you have the opportunity to approach, close in and deal a few attacks before distancing your character again and repeating the same process.
  • During the Network Test, many players have previously called this nameless mini-boss the Many Armed Key Master.
  • This enemy is likely the “Spider” that Roderika speaks about.

Grafted Scion Attacks & Counters

Attack Description Counter
Slash Slashes one or both of its swords in various directions Usually used as part of a combo, so dodge away and wait for the combo to finish
Rolling Slash Swings both its swords in one direction while rolling, then swings them twice again before scuttling back a bit Dodge away twice.
Stab Quickly stabs one or both of its swords forward Dodge to either side
Leaping Stab Leaps high into the air, then stabs its sword straight down into the ground Roll forward a beat after it reaches the apex of its leap as it raises its shield, then get in an attack while it recovers
Shield Slam Lifts its shield high above its head, then slams it down Roll clockwise around it as its body begins to descend and get in an attack while it recovers
Shield Bash Shoves its shield forward Roll back
Crawling Stabs Stands up higher and stabs the ground while walking forward, then leaps and slams down all its weapons Walk around it while it moves, then roll forward when its body begins to fall and get in a hit while it recovers
Retreat Stabs one sword then slashes clockwise with the other while scuttling backwards Dodge back and to the side
Scream Lowers its head and screams, dealing damage to anything closeby. Roll back and keep your distance until it's done. Guarding isn't effective against this attack. If you're quick, you can get in one hit between this attack ending and it starting another combo
Golden Tempering Crosses its swords together, buffing them with Holy damage for one minute. Only used below 50% health, and only in the Chapel of Anticipation and Mt. Gelmir This attack takes almost three full seconds and doesn't do any damage, so use it as an opportunity to get in a few hits
Fencing Stance Stands up tall and holds one sword above it in a fencing stance, preparing to do a powerful attack. Only used below 60% health Try to stay far away to encourage it to use Piercing Stabs, which is easier to punish
Spinning Slash Raises up its lower sword, then spins clockwise four and a half times with swords extended. It will push its shield away from its body when the attack is about to end. Used in Fencing Stance you're nearby Run away when it raises up its lower sword and circle behind it as it spins, then move in as the attack finishes to get in a hit while it recovers
Piercing Stabs Spreads its swords wide, then stabs them forward three times, pauses briefly, stabs seven more times, pauses again, then stabs both swords across one another in front of it. Used in Fencing Stance when you're further away Move behind it when you see it spread its swords and start attacking as soon as you're not at risk of getting hit

: Attack can be parried

Grafted Scions Location

From the entrance to Fringefolk Hero's Grave, head down the ramp, double back, and head down again until you reach a bridge over a gap in the floor. Drop down the near end of this gap and you'll land on a platform. Head east, down a flight of stairs, past a fire-breathing statue, and down another flight of stairs.

You'll find yourself in a room with another bridge across a gap. When you approach the corpse on the far end that's holding an Erdtree's Favor Talisman, one Grafted Scion will drop down on top of it and another will drop on the opposite end of the bridge.

Grafted Scion Lore

Lore Items Related to Grafted Scion

Golden Beast Crest Shield

Shield of dull gold with a beast engraved as its crest. Lighter than 'most greatshields, and subsequently easier to wield.

The beast depicted is Serosh, aged counselor who guides the 'golden lineage.

Ornamental Straight Sword

Slender straight sword patterned after an antique ornament. 'Superior swordsmen prefer to wield one in each hand.

After falling from grace, the dregs of the golden lineage sought power and purpose in the past.

Tree-and-Beast Surcoat

Armor worn by soldiers loyal to Godrick the Grafted.

The surcoat depicts the distant Erdtree and the beast regent, an emblem of the golden lineage. 'Both are symbols of glory now past.

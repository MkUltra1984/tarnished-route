Bonny Gaol is a Location in Elden Ring. The Bonny Gaol is found across a wooden bridge, directly south of Bonny Village in southern Scadu Altus.

Bonny Gaol Map

To reach Bonny Gaol, you first need to reach the Bonny Village Site of Grace. Starting from the Moorth Ruins Site of Grace, go towards the ruins and look for the tilted houses with a large hole in the middle. Carefully head inside and jump down on the edges to reach the bottom. Go towards the northwest where you see a slanted piece of structure, then jump down the balcony near it. Walk behind the building, follow the short tunnel, and use the ladder at the end. This will take you to the well in Bonny Village. From there, use the checkpoint, head south, and cross the bridge. Remain on the path and follow the trail of metal barricades and destroyed cages. The entrance is located on the right side. [See Shadow of the Erdtree Map]

Bonny Gaol Walkthrough

ER Shadow of the Erdtree Bonny Gaol Video Walkthrough

After entering from outside, you'll first encounter the Bonny Gaol site of grace See Shadow of the Erdtree Map and a Summoning Pool statue. Proceeding onwards, you'll find yourself at a massive icy cavern, with jars suspended by chains from the ceiling.

Take the path to the right, and a Jar Innards enemy will emerge up ahead and start attacking you. Once you're by the large metal grate where you can see a currently unreachable item, there will be multiple ways to proceed. To the right of the grate, you can enter a room where a Shadow Undead can be heard grovelling. Shadow Undead that are grovelling, kneeling, or sitting at tables don't attack you unless you attack them. Loot the body it was grovelling over for x8 Frozen Maggot, but watch out for the Jar Innards that will crawl out of an overturned jar to attack you.

After exiting the room, you'll see two hanging jars to the left, below the path you're on. Carefully jump down to the lower jar - it's actually a lift that will lower you to an area with two rooms. The closest room leads to a Shadow Undead that will run at you from behind some boxes, and x3 Frozen Maggot around the room that you can loot. Proceed to the second room, but beware the Jar Innards that will run out of the room as you approach. Entering the room, there will be another Jar Innards that will burst out of a jar to attack you, and a Hefty Cracked Pot that you can loot. Jars that Jar Innards can burst out of are always visibly cracked. Return to the ledge near the jar lift, where you can step on a stone sigil to call it back down. Ride the jar lift back up.

Proceed onwards from the original path, ignoring an open room to the right (you'll return here later), and enter a room with multiple cages. There's a Shadow Undead sitting at a table, as well as a corpse with x10 Bolt that you can loot. Be careful as you approach the corpse, as 3 Shadow Undead will lunge at you. Avoid their grab attacks, as they will latch onto you and bite you, and can drain significant amounts of your HP.

Open the door to your left, entering a room with 3 more Shadow Undead - one of them will lunge at you as you proceed onwards. Next to a lit brazier, there's a hole you can jump down which leads to a dark cave. You can pick up a Broken Rune close to where you landed. There are two ways to proceed - east and west. Head east, where you'll encounter multiple Giant Rat enemies. In the wide area with the big Giant Rat, you can enter a narrow cave to the right to take x4 Smithing Stone [2] from a corpse. Return to the path, continuing on until you reach a ladder you can climb up. At the top of the ladder, watch out for a Jar Innards that will burst out of a cracked jar. Before exiting through the hole in the wall, be sure to grab the Stalwart Horn Charm +2 talisman from a nearby corpse. Now, proceed through the hole in the wall, where you'll find a kneeling Shadow Undead and a Frozen Maggot - this is the room you ignored earlier. Exit the room.

Make your way back to the dark cave area that you jumped down to. This time, head west. As you proceed, you'll find an area where multiple Skeletal Slime enemies will drop from the ceiling. If you look around, you'll see that there are 2 paths to take from this area. Feel free to ignore the northwest path, as you'll just end up in a room with more Skeletal Slimes and just one Broken Rune that you can loot. If you take the south path, you'll encounter even more Skeletal Slimes. Just before you jump through the hole in the brick wall, if you turn around and head north, you'll find another Skeletal Slime waiting to drop from the ceiling, and an Invigorating Cured Meat on a corpse. Now, head through the hole to reach a new, lower area of the gaol, with mounds of countless dead bodies.

Follow the water, proceeding onwards until you reach a large area with light coming in from the ceiling. This area has many Living Jar enemies, both large and small. On the right (south) side of the room, there will be a Lost Ashes of War, behind one of the large stone pillars and in front of a large Living Jar soaking in a waterfall. On the left (north) side of the room, there will be a large Living Jar resting, and a corpse by a grate that you can loot a Broken Rune from. In total, there are x4 Frozen Maggot in the area. Once you're done with this area, head to the east end of the room and proceed through an opening into the next area.

There will be an Innard Meat ahead of you that you can loot, but watch out for a Jar Innards that will burst out of a cracked jar hanging above you. As you head to the north side of the room, a Jar Innards will run out from a broken jar. Kill it, and then to the west side of the room, there will be a broken jar that with a Jar Innards that crawls out as you approach it. The broken jar it crawled out of contains a Raw Meat Dumpling. In total, this room contains x3 Frozen Maggot. Heading into the next room, you'll see a Frozen Maggot, as well as a hanging cracked jar containing yet another Jar Innards. Exit the room, where you'll see the large cavern with hanging jars again.

Take the stairs to the left, where you'll see multiple Shadow Undead up ahead. Once you're at the bottom of the stairs, if you turn left, you'll find a corpse in an open cell that you can loot for a Silver Horn Tender. Towards the ledge, there is a Frozen Maggot you can pick up, and 4 Shadow Undead, of which one of them is standing up and will turn to fight you. Opposite to the ledge, there will be another open cell, this time with a Shadow Undead and x3 Frozen Maggot. Just before you enter the next section, there will be another Shadow Undead that will lunge at you. You'll find a Frozen Maggot in the same room. Proceed to a door that you can open to enter a room with 2 Shadow Undead at a table. Upon exiting the room, you'll find yourself at a ledge where a jar is hanging below you. Get on it and it will start lowering, but instead of getting off the jar, stay on it and it will automatically take you all the way up to another area. If you got off the jar by accident and are now on the bridge, there's a sigil nearby that a Shadow Undead is grovelling over, which you can step on to call the jar lift back down.

If you rode the jar lift up, you'll now be at the top level of Bonny Gaol, where you can pick up the Night Set from a corpse by a guillotine, in front of 2 Shadow Undead. Go up the stairs, but watch out for the Jar Innards waiting for you at the top. Behind it, you can find a ledge with a corpse slumped over it, which you can loot the Shield of Night from. Head back to the ledge that you jumped onto to reach this area. There will be a stationary hanging jar below that you can use to safely jump down, to once again reach the jar lift. This time, get off the jar lift at the bottom, to reach a bridge.

Head across the bridge, where you'll encounter a Shadow Undead that runs and cowers from you, and 2 Jar Innards waiting for you in cracked jars. Once you're across the bridge, you can jump down to an area to the northwest. Follow the path ahead, where you'll find x2 Frozen Maggot and one final Jar Innards waiting to ambush you from an open cell. Head down the stairs to where 2 Shadow Undead are walking around. Just before you cross the golden fog, there will be x2 Frozen Maggot nearby, one of which will be around some breakable crates by a ledge.

You will be fighting Curseblade Labirith here, a boss that wields dual blades and can turn himself invisible. You will receive the Curseblade Meera summon upon defeating this boss. You can visit this page for a strategy on how to beat him.
